🔒 Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data Sentinel-X collects, how we use it, and the controls you have over your information.

Effective Date: September 1, 2025  ·  Version 2.0
01 —

Introduction

Sentinel-X ("we", "our", or "us") is a cybersecurity platform that provides AI-powered email threat detection, forensic analysis, and incident response services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website, application, and services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using Sentinel-X, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please discontinue use of the Service.

02 —

Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign up or log in via Google OAuth, we collect:

  • Your name and email address from your Google profile
  • Profile picture URL (used for display purposes only)
  • Google account identifier (for authentication)

2.2 Email Data Submitted for Analysis

When you use Sentinel-X's email analysis features, we process:

  • Email headers (sender, recipient, routing information, timestamps, SPF/DKIM/DMARC records)
  • Email body content (for phishing and threat detection)
  • Attachments metadata (file names, types, sizes, and hash values)
  • URLs and links contained within emails
🔐 Email data is processed solely for threat analysis and forensic investigation. We do not read, sell, or share the content of your emails for advertising or marketing purposes.

2.3 Usage & Analytics Data

We automatically collect certain technical information when you interact with the Service:

  • Browser type and version, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages visited, features used, and interaction timestamps
  • Error logs and performance metrics

2.4 Threat Intelligence Data

When you perform threat lookups or origin investigations, we may process IP addresses, domain names, and other indicators of compromise (IOCs) that you submit for analysis.

03 —

Google OAuth & API Scopes

Sentinel-X uses Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. We request the following scopes:

Scope Purpose
openid Authenticate your identity via Google
email Retrieve your email address for account creation and role assignment
profile Access your name and profile picture for display within the application
🛡️ We request only the minimum scopes necessary. Sentinel-X does not request access to read, send, or modify your Gmail messages. Email analysis is performed only on content you explicitly submit through our upload or paste features.

Sentinel-X's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

04 —

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To authenticate your account, process email threat analysis, generate forensic reports, and provide threat intelligence lookups
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent unauthorized access, abuse, and security incidents within the platform
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve the accuracy of our AI detection models
  • Communication: To send you security alerts, system notifications, and important service updates
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, sell your personal information to third parties, or use email content for any purpose unrelated to cybersecurity analysis.

05 —

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored and handled as follows:

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Email analysis data (submitted emails, forensic reports, and threat assessments) is retained for the duration of your active case and up to 90 days after case closure, unless you request earlier deletion
  • Usage logs are retained for up to 12 months for security monitoring and service improvement
  • Threat intelligence data (IOCs, IP lookups) may be anonymized and retained to improve our detection capabilities
🗑️ You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@sentinelx.security. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
06 —

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party providers who assist in operating our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data
  • Threat Intelligence Networks: Anonymized and aggregated threat indicators may be shared with cybersecurity intelligence partners to improve global threat detection
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Sentinel-X and its users
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate data protection safeguards

We will never sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any unrelated third parties.

07 —

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-based access controls and authentication enforcement
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure development practices and code review processes
  • Incident response procedures for prompt breach notification

While we take comprehensive measures to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication where possible.

08 —

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities, such as automated decision-making
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@sentinelx.security. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

09 —

Cookies & Tracking

Sentinel-X uses essential cookies and local storage to:

  • Maintain your authentication session
  • Store your preferences and theme settings
  • Remember your active case and workspace state

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may affect service functionality.

10 —

Children's Privacy

Sentinel-X is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

11 —

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via email or an in-app notification. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

Your continued use of Sentinel-X after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
